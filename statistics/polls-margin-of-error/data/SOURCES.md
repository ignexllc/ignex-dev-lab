# Data sources (downloaded / transcribed 2026-10-22)

| File | What | Source |
|---|---|---|
| `house_2022_popular_vote.csv` | 2022 US House national popular vote by party | Clerk of the House, Election Statistics, via the table in [Wikipedia: 2022 United States House of Representatives elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_United_States_House_of_Representatives_elections); official: <https://history.house.gov/Institution/Election-Statistics/Election-Statistics/> |
| `generic_ballot_2022_final.csv` | Final generic-ballot polling averages (FiveThirtyEight, RealClearPolitics) | [FAIR, "In 2022 midterms, media were again misled by generic ballot" (Nov 2022)](https://fair.org/home/in-2022-midterms-media-were-again-misled-by-generic-ballot/). | `aapor_national_signed_error.csv` | Mean signed and absolute error of national presidential polls, 2000–2020 | [AAPOR Task Force on 2020 Pre-Election Polling (2021), Table 3](https://aapor.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/11/AAPOR-Task-Force-on-2020-Pre-Election-Polling_Report-FNL.pdf) |
